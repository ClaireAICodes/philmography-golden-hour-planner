    Field notes

    Shooting Singapore light

    Golden hour is short here

    Near the equator the sun drops fast — golden hour lasts roughly 40 minutes, not the hour-plus you get further north. Arrive 15 minutes early and be shooting when it starts.

    A little cloud is a gift

    Thin cloud at sunset catches colour you never get from a clear sky. Heavy grey cloud kills it. The outlook score above weighs cloud cover against rain risk for exactly this reason.

    Mornings are calmer

    Singapore mornings are usually clearer and the streets are quieter — better for portraits in busy spots like Haji Lane or Chinatown. Evenings give you the warmer, longer shadows.

    Rain is a plan, not a cancellation

    Showers pass in 20–30 minutes. Sheltered spots — MRT underpasses, five-foot ways, Jewel — keep a shoot alive, and wet streets reflect beautifully right after.
